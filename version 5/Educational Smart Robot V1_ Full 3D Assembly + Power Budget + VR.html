Virtual reality (VR) mode

VR puts you inside the computer-generated 3D robot at real size (100 cm). Two ways to use it:

  • VR headset (WebXR): open this page in the headset browser (e.g. Meta Quest Browser) or in a desktop browser with a headset, then press Enter VR headset. Trigger selects a part or wire (its info appears on the panel on your left controller). Left stick drives the robot. Right stick moves you around. X toggles the ghost shell, Y cycles life-size / table-top miniature / giant, grip resets the robot.
  • Cardboard (phone): press Cardboard (phone), allow motion sensors, put the phone in a Cardboard-style viewer. Turn your head to look around. Tap the screen to start / stop the robot driving forward. Exit with the phone's back gesture / Esc. (No lens-distortion correction, so it is an approximation.)
  • WebXR and fullscreen can be blocked when a page is shown inside an embedded frame: open the published link in its own browser tab.
